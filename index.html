R-744 CO2 pressure-temperature

Saturation lookup. Training values, confirm on the OEM chart.

Pink near the triple point. The curve ends at the critical point, 31.0 °C / 87.8 °F.

Superheat is coil-outlet temperature minus saturation temperature at coil-outlet pressure. Use the evaporator outlet, not the compressor.

Limits
Triple point −56.6 °C (−69.9 °F) at 5.18 bar abs / 60 psig. No liquid below this.
Critical point 31.0 °C (87.8 °F) at 73.8 bar abs / 1,070 psia. No condenser above this.
Standstill in a 25 °C (77 °F) room: about 63 bar gauge / 914 psig.
